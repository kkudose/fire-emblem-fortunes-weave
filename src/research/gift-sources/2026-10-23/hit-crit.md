## Source section text

Character-Specific Gift Preferences

While a full list of character likes is extensive, specific preferences can significantly accelerate support gain:
  * Alexandra: Eastern Earrings, Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure; also accepts Horse-related items and Pegasus painting.
  * Anatolia: Bagua Divination set, uncommon/rare books and fashion items; accepts common books and fashion for lower gains.
  * Benditz: Eastern Love Story; accepts board games, military medals, weapons, tactics or military history books.
  * Bertrand: Garum (Act 1 only as Cai).
  * Bonaventure: Crimson Ghosh, Volcano Ghosh, Strategy Manuscript; also likes books.
  * Buccar: Mature Ghosh, Rare Spices, Village Recipes, Volcano Ghosh; also likes fermented drinks, military items, training items.
  * Cai: Arrowhead Gem, Crimson Ghost, Sharp Fishhook; also likes Horse-related items.
  * Catania: Common and Select Coffees, Gaudy Bangle; accepts any "rare item" and paintings.
  * Centurio: Coffee, Spirituality-type items, training items, items for those who "revere martial achievements".
  * Dadao: Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure, Poems of the Greats; likes any Cervi-type drink.
  * Dante: Crimson Ghosh, Magic-Trick set, The Works of Dante; also likes other books and coffee.
  * Diego: Crimson Ghosh, Protective Figurine; also likes archery, board games, and books.
  * Dietrich: Ginji Cervi, Honey Milk, Honey Pastries, Pastry Cookbook, Protection Figurine.
  * Esmeralda: Eastern Earrings, Tales of Adventure; accepts sweet food and fishing-related items.
  * Fabio: Tales of Adventure, Volcano Ghosh; also accepts Coffee items.
  * Fianna: Arrowhead Gem, Blue-Rose Bouquet, Volcano Ghosh; also likes books, coffee, tea, and strong seasoning.
  * Gaitz: Common Coffee, Horse-Grooming Kit, Coffee; also likes other Horse-related items, tea, weapons.
  * Goliath: Strategy Manuscript, Volcano Ghosh; accepts board games and coffee.
  * Guzran: Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure, Volcano Ghosh; accepts other fermented drinks.
  * Halvin: Agricultural items, Non-pickled vegetables, rare southern seeds, magic trick set.
  * Inyoni: Archery and Fishing-related items.
  * Io: Common Coffee, horse-related items.
  * Jasmine: Crimson Ghosh, Dual Fish Knives, Outdoor Cooking set, Rare Spices; also likes board games, cooking items, and garum.
  * Jester: Crimson Ghosh, Dual Fish Knives, Spirit Board Game; also likes fashion items, crafting knives, Honey Pastries, and Saramis sweets.
  * Kiroc: Arrowhead Gem, Select and Southern coffee; accepts fashion items and flowers.
  * Leda: Eastern Black Silk, Eastern Earrings, Tales of Adventure, Volcano Ghosh; really likes fashion items, gemstones, paintings, and items for "lovers of beauty".
  * Lillian: Eastern Earrings, Eastern Tea Leaves, Poems of the Greats, Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure, Blue Rose Bouquets; accepts Fashion items.
  * Loretta: Honey Pastries, Pure-White Cervi, Pastry Cookbook; accepts sweets (mostly pastries).
  * Ludia: Eastern Love Story; accepts other books, coffee, and paintings.
  * Lysander: Dagda Beard Grass, Eastern Tea Leaves, Horse-Grooming Kit, Wing Fletching; accepts Ceremonial Spear, Intimidation Medal, training items.
  * Majide: Fermented drinks (Crimson, Mature, Volcano Ghosh), Dual Fish Knives.
  * Mikaela: Tales of Adventure, Volcano Ghosh; accepts coffee items.
  * Mu: Candy Crystals, Jamel Milk, Weak Cervi, Yarc Milk; accepts any Cervi-type drink, coffee, pastries.
  * Nezha: Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure; accepts board games and vegetables.
  * Ninae: Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure; likes Eastern Black Silk, flowers, paintings.
  * Noctula: Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure; accepts training items.
  * Nuzzuo: Common and Select Coffee, Training Bracelet; likes vegetables, outdoor cooking set, other cooking-related items.
  * Nydine: Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure; accepts books, horse grooming kit, archery items (not Mane ornaments).
  * Olympia: Eastern Earrings, Eastern Tea Leaves, Orgus Coffee, Tales of Adventure; accepts Board games, flowers, paintings, items for "lovers of beauty".
  * Orchel: Agricultural items, books, cooking items (rare spices), paintings, rare southern seeds.
  * Peppe: Board Game Tactics, Outdoor Cooking set, Protection Figure, Sturdy Rucksack; accepts coffee and board games.
  * Peter: Eastern Tea Leaves, Kitten Figurine, Wing Fletching, Yarc Milk; loves pastry cookbook, Cervi-type drinks, most sweets.
  * Seteth: Poems of the Greats, Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure; accepts fishing items, most coffee items, cooking books.
  * Sha Lan: Special "Medicine"; accepts tea, cooking items.
  * Simon: Crimson Ghosh, Dual Fish Knives, Protection Figurine; eats most vegetables.
  * Sirocco: Books, fermented drinks, paintings (Portrait of Yu Phas).
  * Sofia: Cooking items, garum, sweets (Ginji Cervi, Honey Milk, Honey Pastries, Yarc Milk).
  * Talimun: Board games, coffee, fermented drinks, uncommon/rare books.
  * Theodora: Dadga Beard Grass, Eastern Black Silk, Eastern Tea Leaves, Horse-Grooming Kit, Portrait of Yu Phas; accepts coffee, cooking items, fashion, sturdy rucksacks, training items.
  * Tialla: Board games, most books, any Cervi-type drinks.
  * Tobias: Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure, Volcano Ghosh; accepts fermented drinks.
  * Ultand: Blue-Rose Bouquet, Crimson Ghosh, Eastern Love Story, Outdoor Cooking set, Pastry Cookbook; accepts other books, Garum, cooking items.
  * Ursula: Eastern Love Story, Kitten Figurine, Mane Ornament, Pack of Pastels; likes board games, books, coffee, flowers, paintings.
  * Yang Jie: Eastern Tea Leaves, Poem of the Greats, Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure, Volcano Ghosh; likes fermented drinks, books.
  * Zarcone: Eastern Tea Leaves; accepts all vegetables, Eastern Earrings, Gaudy Bangle, Strategy Manuscript, Tales of Adventure, Wing Fletching.
