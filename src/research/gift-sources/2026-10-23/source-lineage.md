# Source lineage notes from the Korean source catalog

- Source: [출처·신뢰도 — 파이어 엠블렘 만자천홍](https://bubae.github.io/fe-fortunes-weave-guide/sources.html)
- Retrieved: 2026-10-23. This page is a source/provenance catalog, not a gift-reaction table; no gift pairs were copied from it.

The catalog's entries relevant to this archive say:

- GameSky is a mirror of Josh Broadwell's Polygon “Best gifts for each character” article.
- Its GameSpot gifts entry is a Yahoo republication, and it groups those character lists with the Game8 source family rather than counting them as independent.
- It lists GameWith's gift/preferences table as a separate Japanese guide source.
- It lists Redfreshet's gift price/store database as a database source.

These are the catalog author's provenance judgments, not independently verified contracts between publishers. The archive uses them as lineage cautions: GameSpot and Game8 are not treated as independent corroboration; GameSky/Polygon and Polygon-derived Ranzware entries are not counted separately; GameWith and Redfreshet remain separate published datasets, without assuming their underlying evidence is independent.
